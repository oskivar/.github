# Oskivar

**Oskivar** is a native AI workspace in active development, designed to bring multi-agent collaboration and computer automation into one platform.

We are building an environment where people can coordinate AI teams to work across development tools, creative applications, office software, and other supported workflows, not just chat or code.

### What we''re building
- **Multi-agent collaboration:** Leaders, optional sub-leaders, and specialized workers collaborating on shared tasks.
- **Cross-device workflows:** Local and connected-device automation with explicit access controls.
- **App integrations:** Extensible capabilities for development, design, productivity, and research.
- **Native foundations:** A Rust-based engine with a modular architecture.

> **Status:** Oskivar is under active development. Features and platform availability may change before release.
